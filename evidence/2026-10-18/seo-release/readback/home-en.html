What should you check before you hand over money?

Confirm the final rate at the counter, the banknote condition and denomination, and the ID requirement. Licensed money changers in Thailand record identification for exchange transactions, so bring your passport.

What do people ask before exchanging THB?

Is it cheaper to exchange cash in Bangkok or at the airport?

City branches are generally cheaper than airport counters. Compare the estimated THB you receive for the same amount before you decide, and remember that airport hours and branch hours are not the same.

Can you exchange USDT or BTC for baht in Thailand?

Yes, through the Thai SEC-licensed exchanges listed on this site. You need a verified account and a THB withdrawal to a bank account; the comparison includes trading fees and THB withdrawal fees where they are known.

Do you need a passport to exchange money in Thailand?

Bring your passport. Licensed money changers record identification for exchange transactions, and larger amounts are checked more carefully.

Why does this site show an estimated amount instead of a final rate?

The rates are observations from public sources, not a guaranteed counter quote. Every amount is an estimate, and the final rate is set by the provider when you exchange.

How often does the data update?

Crypto prices refresh about every 30 seconds while the public feed is healthy. Cash rate references are collected on a scheduled 10-minute cycle and labelled live, hybrid or fallback so you can see how current they are.
